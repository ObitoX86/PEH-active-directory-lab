## Summary

## Preconditions and scope

What permissions, configuration, hosts and lab assumptions were required?

## Reproduction and evidence

Include minimal, redacted steps, relevant output and `evidence/` filenames.

## Security impact

Explain the demonstrated impact and distinguish it from hypothetical outcomes.

## Root cause

Identify the misconfiguration, protocol behaviour or control weakness.

## Recommended remediation

Give specific mitigations appropriate to the lab environment.

## Verification of remediation

Record a follow-up test after applying controls.

## Limitations

State what was not tested or proved.
